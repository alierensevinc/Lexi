YDS Kelime & Soru Bankası

YDS Kelime & Soru Bankası is a comprehensive mobile application designed specifically for YDS exam candidates. It combines 3,301 essential exam vocabulary words with a full 4,224-question YDS Question Bank, serving as your all-in-one English vocabulary and exam preparation assistant.

Features

  • 4,224-Question YDS Question Bank: Practice with 15 official Past YDS Exams (2018–2025), 14 full-length 80-question Practice Exams, 11 topic categories, and targeted drills
  • 180-Minute Exam & Instant Practice Modes: Simulate real timed 180-minute exams with detailed score and category breakdowns, or solve with instant feedback, solutions, and vocabulary glossaries
  • Interactive Flashcards (3,301 Words): Study English-Turkish exam vocabulary with swipeable flashcards and Leitner spaced repetition
  • Multiple Learning Modes: Quiz mode, word matching games, searchable Library, and saved favorites
  • Smart Notifications: Customizable reminders to maintain your study schedule and daily streak
  • Progress Tracking: Monitor your vocabulary mastery and question bank accuracy with detailed statistics
  • 100% Offline Access: Built-in SQLite question bank and local MMKV progress storage let you study anytime, anywhere without an internet connection

Tech Stack

  • React Native 0.88 & Expo SDK 58: Modern New Architecture mobile application with React Compiler
  • Expo SQLite: Pre-bundled, deduplicated offline database for 4,224 YDS questions and reading passages
  • MMKV: Ultra-fast local storage for vocabulary progress, exam attempts, and bookmarks
  • Expo Notifications: Smart study reminder scheduling
  • Expo Router: File-based navigation within the app
  • React Native Reanimated 4 & Gesture Handler 3: 60fps UI-thread animations and gestures

Target Audience

  • YDS Exam Candidates
  • Turkish Speakers Learning English
  • Self-Paced Learners
  • Busy Professionals
